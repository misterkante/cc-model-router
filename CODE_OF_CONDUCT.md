# Code of conduct

[English](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) · [Français](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.fr.md)

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a useful, low-drama place to work. Contributors and
maintainers pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct about code and evidence; be respectful to people.
- Assume good faith, but ask for proof when a claim is measurable.
- Keep criticism about the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy: do not post secrets, private paths, or another person's data.
- Accept that maintainers may reject a change for scope, maintenance cost or
  missing evidence.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, doxxing, unwanted sexual
attention, and publishing private information without permission are not
acceptable. Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews is also not
acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews and
other project spaces, and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report incidents privately to the maintainer through GitHub. If the report
involves the maintainer, use GitHub's abuse reporting flow. Reports will be
reviewed as promptly as possible and handled with discretion. Maintainers may
remove comments, close threads, reject contributions or temporarily or
permanently ban participants.
